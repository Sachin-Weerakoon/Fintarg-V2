1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This Functional Requirements Specification (FRS) defines what the system must do: each function, its inputs,
outputs and rules. It is the detailed companion to the Software Requirements Specification (SRS-001), which
covers scope, users, quality attributes and constraints.
1.2 Product summary
A web platform that helps ordinary people (salaried employees, government workers, business owners) track daily
income and expenses, understand whether they can make it through the month, plan savings, and keep their
personal and business paperwork (documents, letters, agreements) in one place. It is offered in two categories: a
simple Basic version and a fuller Business version.
1.3 How requirements are written
 Field
Meaning
ID
Unique reference, e.g. FR-4.7. Group number = module.
Priority
Must = required for the release; Should = important, include if possible; Could = nice to have.
Tier
Basic = Basic users; Business = Business users only; Both = all users.
Source
S1-S7 = the numbered section of the translated conversation the requirement comes from (see 1.4).
Proposed = not said by the speaker; added by the analyst to make the requirement complete and testable.
Needs stakeholder approval.

3. Calculation Rules and Worked Example
These rules make the analysis testable. The example uses the figures from the conversation.
Rule
Formula
Total outflow
Living expenses + finance payments + loan interest + planned personal spending + savings set
aside
Net position
Total income - total outflow
Shortfall
If net position is below zero, shortfall = the negative amount
Daily-to-monthly goal
Daily amount x days in month (Rs. 1,000 x 30 = Rs. 30,000)
Free cash
Income - living expenses - finance payments - loan interest
Goal feasible?
Yes if free cash is at least the savings target; otherwise warn
Loan interest
Principal x rate x period (simple) or compounding per record setting

  Opens Analysis for the current month.

 System shows net position and remaining money.

  Creates a goal and enters Rs. 1,000 per day.

  Chooses Letters, then template and Personal or Business.

6. Validation and Error Handling
 Area
Rule
Amounts
Numeric, greater than zero, at most two decimals, Rs. only in release 1.
Dates
Valid calendar dates; loan or pawn due date cannot be before its start date.
Contact numbers
Valid Sri Lankan mobile or landline format; up to 3 personal numbers.
NIC
Accepts old (9 digits + V/X) and new (12 digits) formats.
Uploads
Type and size checked before upload; virus scan on the server; clear error message on failure.

Confirmation required for deleting any financial record, goal, document or agreement.
Data loss
Unsaved-changes warning when leaving a form.
